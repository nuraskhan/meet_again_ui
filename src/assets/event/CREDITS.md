# The default pictures of plans

One photograph per kind of activity, shown on a plan that has no photograph of its own
(`src/components/EventPicture.vue`).

All are from Unsplash, taken under the Unsplash License: free to use, also commercially, without
asking and without having to credit (https://unsplash.com/license). Each was fetched from
`images.unsplash.com` cropped to 960 x 540 (JPEG, quality 70). None shows a recognisable person.

The photographers are named on those pages; their names could not be read automatically when the
files were taken, so they are not listed here. Crediting is not required by the licence, but if
the pictures are ever credited in the application, take the names from the pages.

To replace a picture: put another file of the same name here (16:9, about 960 px wide), and
update its row.
